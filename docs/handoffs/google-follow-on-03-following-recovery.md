## Implemented behavior and architectural decisions

[Phase 03 implementation of G08–G11](../architecture.md#phase-03-implementation-of-g08g11) extends G07's previously documented write-path amendment without replacing G01–G07 or allocating new ADR identifiers. G12–G13 remain future work.

- Following edits compile the existing domain split into a conditional head trim/delete followed by a tail create in one durable ordered group. Following deletion omits the tail; a first-occurrence split deletes the empty head. Stable tail resource identity and source shadow are persisted before HTTP. Only the adapter's existing safe provider-only fields copy into the new resource.
- Canonicalization accepts only planner-produced segments of the established all-day/whole-second UTC subset. The domain counts scheduled starts before exceptions, so head COUNT stops strictly before the pivot and tail COUNT retains remaining starts. Reanchoring at a scheduled pivot preserves daily/monthly/yearly interval modulus and Monday weekly phase, including multi-week/multiple-weekday rules. Inclusive endings and invalid-date skips remain exact. Preview comparisons supplement this field/phase argument; they never authorize unknown patterns. Arbitrary imported segments and regional/DST/ordinal/fractional schedules remain unsupported.
- Every Google following edit/delete explicitly confirms resetting future exceptions, even if the cache has none. Renderer editor/gestures and CLI updates/deletions carry reset consent; `--reset-exceptions` is required with following deletion. Preceding exceptions survive; incoming exceptions outside the trimmed schedule are excluded from effective state. Whole-series metadata edits preserve exceptions and structural whole-series edits remain blocked.
- Restart and uncertain-response recovery resume forward with persisted identities, operation markers, revisions and acknowledgements. A successful trim remains effective while the tail is pending. Complete local overlays remain visible until the group completes or is explicitly resolved. Successful steps never trigger automatic rollback; unrelated work continues after group-local permission/validation failures, and authentication retains the existing account-wide pause.
- Account recovery exposes completed/total steps for incomplete groups, including blocked partial actions. Conflict review lists ordered resource steps and explains partial Keep Google/discard results. Superseded steps retain durable audit/dependency rows but do not count as successful progress.
- Checked Keep local rebases persisted dirty metadata/occurrence fields against observations. Combined master/exception and multiple-exception intent expands into ordered master/instance steps, retaining existing exceptions during the master step. For grouped actions it replaces only the conflicting resource's unfinished steps inside the existing group, preserving acknowledged siblings and other resources' uncertain identities. Replacements precede the remaining tail; later overlapping groups retain overlays and receive corrected dependencies rather than creating cycles. Stale reviews fail. Changed schedules that cannot retain the boundary, and occupied reserved create identities, fail safely instead of silently overwriting Google.
- Keep Google retires the connected pending remainder and restores retained incoming state, including a successfully trimmed head without a tail. Checked calendar/account discard retains its existing worker fence and successful-remote-step warning. It does not compensate delivered requests.
- Unattempted complete groups undo atomically. Attempted multi-step groups must finish forward reconciliation with Retry/conflict resolution before Undo queues checked ordered compensation. Compensation restores the master and known exceptions and conditionally deletes the tail; deleted masters use fresh IDs. Session history still ends at restart. Resetting uncached future exceptions cannot be reversed from the cache; the editor warns that Undo restores only cached changes.

## Schema/API and compatibility

Schema remains v6. Existing group, member snapshot, ordered outbox, reserved identity, revision dependency and acknowledgement fields persist the entire split/replan. Superseded steps use acknowledgement JSON rather than a new state or migration. Existing pending-work migration/restart tests remain applicable. Downgrading to pre-v6 remains unsupported.

Generated native TypeScript contracts add optional `AccountSyncStatus.recoveryGroups` (`GroupProgress`) and `SyncConflict.recoverySteps` (`GroupRecoveryStep`). The existing opaque group review fingerprint covers all observations, overlays, dependencies and acknowledgements. `EventMutation` retains its phase 02 shape: native code selects reset for confirmed following mutations and preserve for Google occurrence/series mutations. CLI deletion gains `--reset-exceptions`. Credentials, provider boundaries and pure domain contracts remain unchanged.

## Remaining restrictions and next phase

Structural whole-series schedule changes, arbitrary segment schedules and regional/DST expansion remain gated by exact correspondence/equivalence evidence. Remote moves, attendees/meetings, conference creation and special Google event writes remain unsupported. Attempted multi-step Undo requires completed forward reconciliation, rather than cancelling unknown remote effects. An occupied reserved create ID or incompatible concurrent schedule requires review/Keep Google and a fresh action. Only known cached exceptions can be restored after a confirmed future reset.

Next is Google follow-on 04: durable full-account removal with lifecycle/credential fencing and checked cleanup. Carry forward everyone/external-only/none notification choices with everyone as the future default, and keep phase-specific live acceptance distinct from historical validation.
